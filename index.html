Backup and restore

Your data lives only on this device. Save a backup file so you can restore it later.
Without a passphrase the backup is only scrambled, so it is unreadable at a glance but not secure. With one, it is properly encrypted and can only be restored with the same passphrase. If you forget it, that backup cannot be recovered.

Getting started
Add an entryExpense, income or invest
  1. Tap Add entry to open the form.
  2. Pick Expense, Income or Invest at the top of the form.
  3. Choose a category, and a subcategory if you use them.
  4. Type the amount on the keypad.
  5. Tap Add. Account, date and note are optional.
Calculate while addingUse the keypad as a calculator
  1. Open the amount keypad.
  2. Use + , − , × and ÷ between numbers.
  3. The amount field shows the result as you type.
  4. Tap = to finish, then Add.
TagsGroup entries your way
  1. Press and hold an entry.
  2. Pick a tag, or tap + New tag to make one.
  3. Tap anywhere outside to close.
  4. See totals per tag on the Stats tab.
Money
Credit card billsDue dates and payments
  1. Add the card under Accounts with its bill day and grace days.
  2. A banner appears when the bill is generated and again as the due date nears.
  3. Tap Paid on the banner to log the payment.
RefundsFull or partial
  1. Tap the entry and choose Refund.
  2. Enter the full amount, or a smaller one for a partial refund.
  3. The refunded part moves out of your spending; the rest stays.
TransfersMove money between accounts
  1. Open Accounts and choose Transfer.
  2. Pick the from and to accounts and the amount.
  3. Transfers are not counted as spending.
AutopayEntries that repeat by themselves
  1. Open Accounts and add an Autopay.
  2. Choose expense, income or invest, then the frequency and day.
  3. Entries are added on their dates when you open the app.
BudgetsKeep spending in check
  1. Set a monthly budget under Settings.
  2. Mark an entry as not in budget to leave it out.
  3. Stats shows what is left and your daily average.
Your data
Backup and restoreKeep your data safe
  1. Open the menu and choose Backup and restore.
  2. Save the file in Drive or send it to yourself.
  3. To restore on a new phone, open the same page and pick the file.
Export reportsExcel, CSV or PDF
  1. Open the menu and choose Export reports.
  2. Pick the period, type, account, category and tag.
  3. Choose the file type and tap Download report.
Hide amountsPrivacy in public
  1. Open Appearance and turn on Hide balances and totals.
  2. Tap the eye icon to show them for a moment.
  3. Entry amounts stay visible.
